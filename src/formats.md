# Question formats used in the real SOF Class 1 papers

Observed across the 2026-27 sample papers for IGKO, IMO, ISO, ICSO and IEO
(10 questions each, 50 in total — these are the only free papers SOF publishes).

This file records **formats and structure only**. SOF's actual questions are
their copyright and are deliberately not reproduced here. Every question in
this app is written from scratch against the published syllabus.

---

## Formats the app already produces

| Format | Seen in | Example shape |
|---|---|---|
| Plain 4-option MCQ | all | "Which is our national bird?" |
| Odd one out, on a stated basis | ISO, ICSO | "...on the basis of number of legs" |
| Ranking / position in a row | ISO, ICSO | "7th from the left end" |
| Day reasoning | ICSO | "If today is X, the day after tomorrow is..." |
| Word analogy | IMO, ISO | "A is to B as C is to ?" |
| Grouping into equal sets | IMO, ISO | "4 groups of ___ each" |
| Expanded form of a number | IMO | "10 + 6" |
| 3D solids | IMO | cube / sphere / cylinder / cuboid |
| Word problems with Indian names | IMO | "Aarohi has… she gave…" |

## Formats the app does NOT yet produce — text-based, so buildable

| Format | Seen in | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Select the INCORRECT option** | IGKO, ICSO | Inverted logic. Trips children who skim. |
| **Select the INCORRECT match** | IGKO | Pairs like person–sport, one pair wrong |
| **Give a group name for a set** | IGKO | "Laptop, Tablet, Webcam, Smartphone" → Gadgets |
| **Unscramble the letters** | ICSO | Jumbled word, choose the device |
| **First letters of pictures make a word** | ICSO | Needs pictures, but the mechanic is text |
| **Match Column-P with Column-Q** | IMO | Answered as one of four combinations |
| **True/False statement pairs** | IMO | "(p)… (q)… → T/T, T/F, F/T, F/F" |
| **Reading comprehension passage** | IEO | Short story, then questions about it |
| **Fill in the blank (grammar)** | IEO | Tense, preposition, article |
| **Identify part of speech** | IEO | Underlined word → noun/verb/adjective/preposition |
| **Choose the correct spelling** | IEO | Four spellings, one right |
| **Complete a conversation** | IEO | Two speakers, choose the natural reply |

## Things the papers assume that this app did not

- **IGKO is not India-only.** The sample paper asks about the Statue of Liberty
  and offers Paris / London / Berlin. "India **and the World**" is literal.
- **Current Affairs includes Indian politics** — e.g. naming a sitting Chief
  Minister. This cannot be answered by a static question bank without going
  stale, and needs re-checking every cycle.
- **Sports means named people matched to their sport**, not just rules.
- **Yoga and Surya Namaskar** appear under Indian culture.
- **ICSO is Windows 11 specific**, by SOF's own note, and its IT section covers
  gadgets, apps and robots — not just the parts of a desktop PC.
- **IEO vocabulary is hard.** The Class 1 sample paper uses words like
  *jubilation*, *suffocating*, *appreciation* and *trowel*, and tests tense
  agreement after "didn't". It is markedly harder than the GK paper.
