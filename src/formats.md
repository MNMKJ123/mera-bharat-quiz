# Question formats used in the real SOF Class 1 papers

Observed across the 2026-27 sample papers for IGKO, IMO, ISO, ICSO and IEO
(10 questions each, 50 in total — these are the only free papers SOF publishes).

This file records **formats and structure only**. SOF's actual questions are
their copyright and are deliberately not reproduced here. Every question in
this app is written from scratch against the published syllabus.

## Things the papers assume that this app did not

- **IGKO is not India-only.** The sample paper asks about the Statue of Liberty
  and offers Paris / London / Berlin. "India **and the World**" is literal.
- **Current Affairs includes Indian politics** — e.g. naming a sitting Chief
  Minister. This cannot be answered by a static question bank without going
  stale, and needs re-checking every cycle.
- **Sports means named people matched to their sport**, not just rules.
- **Yoga and Surya Namaskar** appear under Indian culture.
- **ICSO is Windows 11 specific**, by SOF's own note, and its IT section covers
  gadgets, apps and robots — not just the parts of a desktop PC.
- **IEO vocabulary is hard.** The Class 1 sample paper uses words like
  *jubilation*, *suffocating*, *appreciation* and *trowel*, and tests tense
  agreement after "didn't". It is markedly harder than the GK paper.
